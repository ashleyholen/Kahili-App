OUR STORY

The cultural and sustainable significance of our brand

Kāhili, a traditional feather standard created for Ali'i and/or family of the Hawaiian Islands to represent mana, instilling a sense of dignity and regal presence. To create a Kāhili takes thousands of feathers, which are plucked in small amounts from birds of Hawai'i, called a release method. This method is used to sustainably catch a bird, gather a couple of feathers, release and catch another, to keep the feather source in Hawai'i stable, rather than taking all the feathers of a single bird. The representation of a Kāhili, and it's sustainable building process, is a symbol that is suited to bring awareness, and set a baseline of the possible ways to save Hawai'i.

MISSION STATEMENT

Kāhili is an app designed to foster sustainable tourism in Hawaiʻi by providing cultural insights and actions for conservation, empowering tourists to actively contribute to the preservation of Hawaii's natural and cultural heritage.
